Privacy Policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant application used exclusively by its developer for private, non-commercial purposes. It is not distributed to or used by the public.

Data accessed

With explicit authorization, Jarvis accesses the operator's own Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Google Docs to carry out actions the operator requests directly.

Data storage

All credentials and data are stored locally on the operator's own device, encrypted using the operating system's built-in secure storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server controlled by the developer.

Third parties

Voice requests are processed via the Anthropic Claude API and Google's own APIs solely to fulfill the operator's requests. No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used for any purpose beyond the operator's own use of the app.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the developer at the support email listed on this app's Google OAuth consent screen.
